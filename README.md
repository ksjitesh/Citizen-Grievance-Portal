# CivicAI — AI-Powered Citizen Grievance Portal

> Intelligent grievance registration, AI-assisted classification, smart routing, priority assistance, duplicate detection, and resolution tracking.

CivicAI is a citizen grievance management platform designed to make civic complaint reporting and administrative processing more structured, transparent, and efficient.

The platform allows citizens to report civic problems such as potholes, garbage accumulation, broken streetlights, drainage issues, water supply problems, damaged infrastructure, and other public-service concerns.

CivicAI uses an AI-assisted processing layer to analyze grievance information, suggest categories and departments, identify potential duplicate reports, generate priority indicators, and provide a structured workflow for authority review.

# 📌 Problem Statement

Citizens regularly encounter civic problems such as:

- Road damage and potholes
- Garbage accumulation
- Broken streetlights
- Water leakage and supply issues
- Drainage problems
- Traffic and transportation issues
- Public infrastructure damage
- Environmental concerns
- Public safety issues

Reporting the problem is only the first part of the process.

Administrative teams may still need to:

1. Understand unstructured complaint descriptions.
2. Identify the appropriate category.
3. Determine the responsible department.
4. Identify similar or duplicate complaints.
5. Prioritize incoming cases.
6. Review location information.
7. Track case progress.
8. Communicate status changes to citizens.

A poorly structured grievance workflow can make complaint handling slower and make it difficult for citizens to understand what is happening after submission.
